Are young drivers being fined more than other age groups?

01 — Age Group

Who is getting fined the most?

Comparing fine counts across age groups and offence types across Australia. Select an offence type below to explore the data.

💡 Key Insight: Drivers aged 40–64 receive the highest total fines overall, however drivers aged 17–25 are disproportionately represented given their smaller share of the total driving population — suggesting young drivers pose a higher per-capita road risk.
✅
Recommendation: Road safety authorities should implement targeted enforcement campaigns and awareness programs specifically aimed at drivers aged 17–25, particularly around speeding and mobile phone use, to reduce fine rates in this high-risk group.

Which Australian states have the highest road safety enforcement activity?

04 — By State

How do states compare?

Total fines ranked by Australian state — filter by age group and offence type to explore patterns.

💡 Key Insight: NSW and VIC consistently lead in total fines due to their larger populations and higher traffic volumes, while smaller states like ACT and TAS record significantly lower enforcement numbers.
✅
Recommendation: Smaller states should benchmark against NSW and VIC's enforcement strategies to identify gaps in road safety coverage. Population-adjusted fine rates should be used to more accurately compare enforcement effectiveness across states.
