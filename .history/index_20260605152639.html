⚠️ Data Governance Notes
Northern Territory: In 2023, both roadside drug tests conducted and positive tests were not provided. Only positive drug test results were reported.
Tasmania: Did not report breath test data for 2023–2024 and is excluded from breath test comparisons.
Location Data: NT, QLD, TAS and WA did not report location breakdowns — only NSW, VIC, SA and ACT have location-level data.
Unknown Age Group: Large numbers of camera-detected offences in NSW, QLD and VIC have no associated age data and are excluded from age group charts.

Data sourced from the Bureau of Infrastructure and Transport Research Economics (BITRE), 2024.
